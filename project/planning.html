Planning & Safety

Use this checklist and share trail suggestions or conditions with the TrailNest community.

Packing checklist (quick)

  • Water + snacks
  • Layers (weather changes fast)
  • Sun protection (hat, sunglasses, sunscreen)
  • Charged phone + offline map
  • Basic first aid
  • Trash bag (pack it out)

Seasonal notes

Spring: Mud, runoff, and slick morning ice in shaded areas.

Summer: Start early, watch for lightning, bring extra water.
